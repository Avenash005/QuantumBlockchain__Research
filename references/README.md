## Quantum machine learning review

Devadas, R. M., & T, S. (2025). Quantum machine learning: A comprehensive review of integrating AI with quantum computing for computational advancements. *MethodsX, 14*, 103318.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mex.2025.103318
- Scope: quantum-enhanced classical ML and native QML; SVM, KNN, quantum neural networks; quantum feature maps, distance metrics, circuit design, applications, and limitations.
- Research gaps discussed: noisy hardware, error correction, limited data encoding, scalability, training/resource costs, and generalization beyond small benchmark datasets.
- Note: use as a survey and follow its cited primary studies for detailed claims.
