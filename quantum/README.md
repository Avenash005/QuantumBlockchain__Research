# Quantum Computing Foundations

**Author:** Avenash V

This section documents the foundations of quantum information. It begins with classical binary information and progresses through quantum physics, qubits, superposition, measurement, and entanglement.

| Part | Focus | Key ideas |
|---|---|---|
| 01 — Classical computing | Bits and binary information | Binary representation, logic, information |
| 02 — Quantum physics | Quantum behaviour and probability | Wave-particle duality, double-slit experiment, interference |
| 03 — Qubits | Quantum states | Basis states, amplitudes, normalization |
| 04 — Superposition | Linear combinations | Amplitudes, probabilities, interference |
| 05 — Measurement | Outcomes and probabilities | Born rule, measurement basis, state update |
| 06 — Entanglement | Joint quantum states | Separability, Bell states, correlations, Bell's theorem |

## Current milestone: entanglement

The material currently stops at quantum entanglement. Future subjects such as quantum gates, circuits, algorithms, and hardware will be documented in later learning stages.

## Core notation

- \(|0\rangle\), \(|1\rangle\): computational basis states.
- \(\alpha\), \(\beta\): complex probability amplitudes.
- \(|\psi\rangle\): ket notation for a quantum state.
- \(\langle\psi|\): corresponding bra.
- \(|\psi\rangle\otimes|\phi\rangle\): joint product state.
