# Quantum Computing and Blockchain Research

## Research objective

Investigate how advances in quantum computing may affect cryptographic systems used by blockchain networks, and study post-quantum cryptographic approaches that may help protect future systems.

## Research questions
- Which cryptographic primitives used in blockchain systems are relevant to quantum threat analysis?
- What do known quantum algorithms imply for public-key and symmetric cryptography?
- What assumptions are used in published resource estimates?
- Which post-quantum standards and migration strategies are relevant?
- How could blockchain protocols manage cryptographic agility and migration?

## Planned sections
- Blockchain architecture and consensus fundamentals
- Hash functions, digital signatures, and public-key cryptography
- Quantum threat models and resource estimates
- Post-quantum cryptography and standardization
- Migration constraints and open research questions

## Research discipline

Do not describe a theoretical algorithm as a practical attack without evidence. Distinguish mathematical results, laboratory demonstrations, resource estimates, and attacks on deployed systems. Cite primary papers and standards.

The `quantum/` directory builds the foundations needed to read quantum-computing literature. This folder applies that background to cryptographic and blockchain questions.
